Membership FAQs

Answers To Common Questions

Everything you need to know about joining Stackly Sports Club.

Can I freeze or pause my membership if I travel?

Yes. Members on Annual and Pro tiers may freeze their membership up to 60 days per calendar year without forfeiting their seniority or court booking privileges.

Are guest day passes available for visiting friends?

Absolutely. Day passes can be purchased for ₹650 per guest, granting full access to open courts and the swimming arena when accompanied by an active member.

What equipment is provided with court reservations?

All court nets, basketballs, and soccer balls are provided complimentary. Tournament-grade Yonex feather shuttlecocks and Dunlop tennis balls are available for purchase at the front desk pro-shop.
