Facility Science

Anti-Skid Friction & Subfloor Maintenance

The health of an indoor athlete depends on consistent friction coefficients and shock absorption. Our BWF and FIBA timber subfloors undergo rigorous daily thermal and mechanical inspections.

Every wooden court is treated with Swedish Bona Sportive two-component polyurethane varnish, engineered specifically to deliver a certified dynamic coefficient of friction between 0.5 and 0.7—allowing decisive deceleration without abrupt ankle torquing.

0.58 μ Friction Index

Calibrated for explosive pivots, lunges, and recovery steps.

Hygrometric Control

Maintained between 45%–55% relative humidity to eliminate timber swelling.
