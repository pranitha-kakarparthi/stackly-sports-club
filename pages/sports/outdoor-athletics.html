Night Athletics

500 Lux Broadcast Stadium Floodlighting

High-intensity night athletics demand precise, shadow-free illumination. Our outdoor complex is powered by customized Musco LED luminaires calibrated to eliminate upward sky glow and direct light specifically across competition zones.

With a color rendering index (CRI) exceeding 85 and 1,000 FPS slow-motion flicker-free certification, athletes experience crystal-clear sightlines on high-speed football crosses, baseline tennis volleys, and sprint finishes.

Raw Conditioning

Outdoor Sand Dunes & Sprint Incline Ramp

Championship speed is built through resisted acceleration. Adjoining our 400m synthetic track is our dedicated 35-meter 15° inclined sprint ramp and graded beach-sand conditioning pit.

Training on natural loose silica sand recruits stabilizer muscles in the ankles, calves, and hips while eliminating impact stress on knees and spinal discs. Perfect for plyometrics, explosive broad jumps, and resisted parachute sprints.

15° Incline Ramp

Overload propulsion force and triple extension biomechanics.

Impact-Free Plyos

Heavy eccentric buffering protects joints during jump training.
