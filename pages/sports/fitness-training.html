Injury Prevention

Functional Movement Screening & Kinetic Mobility

Peak performance begins with structural symmetry. Before our athletes touch heavy barbell loads, our sports physiotherapists conduct a comprehensive 7-point Functional Movement Screen (FMS) to identify hidden kinetic chain restrictions.

Our dedicated joint decompression studio features inversion gravity tables, pneumatic traction units, and foam myofascial tools designed to restore spinal disc hydration and hip capsule rotation after high-impact training cycles.

Rapid Recovery

Hydro-Thermal Contrast Circuit & Ice Plunge

Accelerate muscle repair and reset your autonomic nervous system with Stackly's clinical contrast hydrotherapy suite. Alternate between cryogenic cold water immersion and magnesium-infused thermal soaks.

Vasoconstriction from our 10°C ice plunge flushes accumulated lactic acid and metabolic bi-products out of fatigued muscles, while the 38°C Epsom salt mineral pool induces vasodilation and flood tissues with oxygen-rich blood.
