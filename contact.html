Frequently Asked Inquiries

Quick answers regarding admissions, court etiquette, and evaluations.

Are trial sessions available before purchasing an annual membership?

Yes. We offer single-day all-access passes for prospective members. During this session, you may tour the arena, test our courts, and complete a movement screen with our head trainers.

What is the court reservation cancellation policy?

Court reservations can be rescheduled or cancelled via the member dashboard up to 2 hours prior to scheduled play without any court-hour penalty.

Is non-marking footwear mandatory across indoor courts?

Yes, strictly. To preserve the shock-absorbing polyurethane varnish and natural wood grain of our BWF and FIBA courts, non-marking indoor gum-rubber soles are required.
