Our Founding Legacy

A Decade of Elevating Athletic Aspirations

Founded in 2012 by former international decathletes and sports physicians, Stackly Sports & Athletic Club began with an uncompromising mission: to eliminate the gap between grassroots athletic passion and world-class training standards.

Over the past decade, our campus at MMR Complex in Chinna Thirupathi, Salem has evolved into a 22-acre flagship destination. We currently train over 12,000 active athletes, ranging from junior prodigies competing on the BWF and ITF circuits to masters-level marathon runners and corporate professionals.
